title: Gallery Mosaic
description: 'Photo mosaic where chosen images span two columns and two rows, giving a gallery some rhythm without a lightbox.'
overview: 'Use Gallery Mosaic when a page needs a lot of photography and a flat grid would feel monotonous. Photos come from the product catalogue (`src/data/products.json`) by collection, featured flag or specific ids, so photography and alt text are never restated — or list them inline when the gallery is not about products. Every photo tiles to a square; `featureEvery` gives every nth tile a two-by-two span so the grid has some rhythm, and inline photos can still flag individual features by hand. Photos are dealt out one product at a time, so a product with six photos does not clump; cap it with `perProduct`. Captions use the product name and sit over the bottom of a tile. The mosaic drops to two columns below 900px and a single column below 520px, where feature tiles level out with the rest.'
