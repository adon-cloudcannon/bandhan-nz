description: 'Large swipeable photo view with a thumbnail strip, driven straight from the product catalogue.'
overview: 'A thin wrapper around Image Carousel that takes its photos from `src/data/products.json` rather than a hand-written list, so alt text and photography are never restated. Choose a collection, the featured products, or specific ids. `perProduct` defaults to 1 so a product with a dozen photos cannot take over the whole carousel — raise it when you want to show a colour range. Use it above a Gallery Mosaic to give a page one big hero photo view and one dense grid.'
